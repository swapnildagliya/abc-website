Privacy

Short and honest

This is a small static website. Here is everything it does — and doesn’t do — with your data.

No cookies

We don’t set cookies and we don’t use browser storage. No analytics, no ad trackers, nothing that measures or logs your visit.

What loads from elsewhere

  • Photos are self-hosted on this site — nothing loads from Squarespace’s image hosting.
  • Typefaces are self-hosted on this site — nothing loads from Google Fonts.
  • The free Bollywood course shows a plain YouTube thumbnail for each lesson — nothing loads from YouTube until you press play. It then opens in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).

YouTube may see your IP address as part of delivering that content, under its own privacy policy, once you choose to press play. We don’t add any tracking of our own on top.

Getting in touch

This site never asks for your email address on a form or a mailto link. Every enquiry — bookings, workshops, classes, festival questions — goes through Shoonya Dance Centre’s contact form, our home studio in Ghent. What you submit there is handled by Shoonya, not stored by this site.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, reach us through the contact form and we’ll answer directly.
