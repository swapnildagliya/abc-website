The story

A Belgian Indian dance company, rooted in Ghent

ABC a bollywood company is a Belgian Indian dance company, based in Ghent and led by Swapnil Dagliya. We perform Bollywood, Bhangra, Garba, Lavani and Indian folk — on theatre stages, at festivals, and at weddings and corporate events. Swapnil founded ABC in Ghent in 2017; since then the company has performed more than 40 times across Europe.

Indian dance is not one thing. Folk, Bollywood and semi-classical each carry their own history, and we move between them on purpose rather than folding everything into a single act. Everybody is welcome in a class — but the form isn't watered down to get them there.

Swapnil Dagliya is from Pune. An IT engineer by training, he studied contemporary dance and ballet at the Broadway Dance Center in New York and Opus Ballet in Florence. He has taught and performed Indian dance since 2008, first running his own school and a dance festival in India.

He co-founded Shoonya Dance Centre in Ghent and founded ABC there in 2017. What began as one teacher became a company. In 2023 he started the Gent India Dans Festival, and ABC now tours workshops across Europe — Brussels, Paris, Berlin, Amsterdam and Prague.

Swapnil holds a diploma in Indian folk dance and has trained in different styles including but not limited to Ghoomar, Chari, Tera Taali, Ghantu & Khoriya. He teaches Bhangra and the Gujarati folk dance Garba.
