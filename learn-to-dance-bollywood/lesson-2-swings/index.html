Lesson 2: Swings

Lesson 2: Swings

IN THIS LESSON

Learn the move:
Swings

The ‘Swing/s’ is a dance move that draws inspiration from the energetic and joyful Bhangra dance, bringing a touch of traditional Indian dance to a simple yet expressive move. This move involves a side-to-side movement of the body, reminiscent of the lively steps in Bhangra, accompanied by a rhythmic swinging of the arms. The ‘Swing/s’ move captures the spirit of Bhangra - the joy, the energy, and the rhythm, while being adaptable to various music genres and dance styles. Remember, dance is about expressing yourself and having fun, so feel free to add your own flair to the ‘Swing/s’ move. Enjoy the rhythm and keep swinging!
