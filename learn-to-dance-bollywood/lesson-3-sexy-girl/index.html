Lesson 3: Sexy Girl

Lesson 3: Sexy Girl

IN THIS LESSON

Learn the move:
Sexy Girl

The ‘Sexy Girl’ is a dance move that draws inspiration from the traditional folk dance Gidda, but with a modern twist. This move captures the spirit of a confident and radiant girl, dancing with a sense of freedom and joy. The signature of this move is the dynamic hair toss, where the dancer swings her long ponytail in circles, reminiscent of the swirling movements in Gidda. This hair flick, combined with rhythmic body movements and footwork, brings a contemporary feel to the traditional dance form. The ‘Sexy Girl’ move is not just about the physical movements, but also about the attitude - it’s about dancing with self-assurance, grace, and a sense of fun. Remember, dance is a form of self-expression, so feel free to put your own spin on the ‘Sexy Girl’ move. Enjoy the rhythm and dance like nobody’s watching. Happy dancing!
