Lesson 5: Shoulder Step

IN THIS LESSON

Learn the move:
Shoulder Step

The Shoulder Step is an energetic Bollywood move. This move involves rhythmic up and down movements of the shoulders, often coordinated with lively footwork and expressive hand gestures. It’s a staple in many Bollywood routines, adding a touch of jazz and funk dance to the upbeat tempo of modern music. When performed correctly, the Shoulder Step can bring a dynamic energy to any dance routine, making it a favourite at parties and celebrations. Remember, the key to mastering this step, like any dance move, is practice and enjoyment. Happy dancing!
